# Pore Salon Simulator — Delete your account

**App:** Pore Salon Simulator · **Provided by:** Go We Are

## Delete it in the Game

1. Open the Game and tap the gear (Settings) at the top right of the first screen.
2. Tap **Back up progress**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## If you cannot open the Game

Email us from, or tell us, the address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. We will delete the account after confirming it is yours.

- Email: go.we.are.official@gmail.com (subject e.g. "Delete my account")

## What is deleted and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your account, backed-up progress (stars, best scores, coins, cosmetics, awards) and your weekly ranking rows. Any remaining coins are lost and cannot be restored.
- **Kept:** purchase records (product, time of purchase, store transaction ID) are unlinked from the account and kept for 5 years as required by law, then deleted.
- Progress made without signing in exists only on your device and is removed when you delete the Game. To remove ranking rows submitted without signing in, email us at the address above.
