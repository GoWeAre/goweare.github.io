# Pore Salon Simulator — Delete your account

**App:** Pore Salon Simulator · **Provided by:** Go We Are

## Delete it in the Game (Android/iOS app)

1. Open the Game and tap Settings (the gear) or **MY**.
2. Tap **Link account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, type "DELETE" in the confirmation box and confirm.

## Version inside the Toss app (Apps in Toss)

The version inside the Toss app has no account deletion menu. Email us at the address below with the subject "Toss version account deletion". To confirm it is you, tell us the nickname shown on the Game's MY screen and roughly when you last played. We will delete the account after checking.

## If you cannot open the Game

Email us from, or tell us, the address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. We will delete the account after confirming it is yours.

- Email: go.we.are.official@gmail.com (subject e.g. "Delete my account", "Toss version account deletion")

## What is deleted and what is kept

- **Deleted:** your account, saved progress (stars, best scores, coins, cosmetics, awards, rename tickets), your weekly ranking and nickname records, and your invite code. Any remaining coins and rename tickets are lost and cannot be restored.
- **Kept, changed so they no longer identify you:**
  - Purchase records (product, time of purchase, transaction ID) are unlinked from the account and kept for 5 years as required by law, then deleted.
  - The invite ledger, so that rewards cannot be claimed again.
  - Nickname report records.
- Progress made without signing in exists only on your device and is removed when you delete the Game. To remove ranking rows submitted without signing in, email us at the address above.
