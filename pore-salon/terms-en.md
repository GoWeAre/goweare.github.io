These are the terms for **Pore Salon Simulator** (the "Game"), provided by **Go We Are** ("we"). The Game is offered as an Android/iOS app (the "mobile app") and as a version that runs inside the Toss app (Apps in Toss, the "Toss version"). These terms apply to both.

**Effective date:** 2026-11-11

## 1. Using the Game

- The Game is free to play. Every stage can be played without paying.
- **Hearts:** starting a stage uses one heart.
  - You can hold up to 10 hearts, and one refills every 15 minutes.
  - Chapter 1 and the Daily Guest do not use hearts.
  - You can also refill hearts by watching an ad or spending coins.
- **Accounts**
  - Mobile app: linking an account (Google or Apple sign-in) is optional. Without it, your progress is stored only on your device and is lost if you delete the Game.
  - Toss version: an account is created automatically from the user key Toss issues, and your progress is saved automatically.
- **Ads:** the Game shows ads. Interstitial ads appear between stages, when restarting Endless, and when leaving Practice after more than a minute. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one.
- **No abuse:** do not tamper with records, change the device clock to gain rewards, abuse payments or rewards, or modify the program. We may remove or reverse records, coins or rewards obtained this way.

  - All items are kept on your Toss version account and can be used on other devices with the same Toss account.
  - If you paid but the item did not arrive, restart the Game or tap "Restore purchases" on the coin screen. Our server checks the order with Toss and then adds the item.

## 3. Withdrawal and refunds

- **Withdrawal:** you may withdraw from a purchase within 7 days.
  - **Coins and rename tickets you have already used cannot be withdrawn** (they are consumable). If you have spent part of a pack, withdrawal of that pack may be limited.
  - Remove Ads takes effect as soon as you buy it. After that, withdrawal follows the refund steps below.
- **Mobile app refunds:** the store processes the payment, so request refunds from the store where you bought the item.
  - Google Play: Play Store app → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, then choose the order and request a refund.
  - App Store: choose the purchase at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com> and request a refund.
  - ONE store: ONE store processes the payment, so request a refund from ONE store customer service. You can also contact us (section 6) and we will walk you through it.
- **Toss version refunds:** in the Toss app's payment history, tap "Get a refund" on the purchase. We check whether the item was used and approve or decline the request. Once approved, it is processed under your device's app store policy, and Toss tells you the result. You can also contact us (section 6).
- **What a refund takes back**
  - We remove the coins and rename tickets from that purchase from your account, down to zero if the balance is lower.
  - Refunding Remove Ads brings interstitial ads back.
  - On the Toss version, this takes effect the next time you start the Game or tap "Restore purchases".
- **Purchases by minors:** if a minor made a purchase without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian may cancel it. Ask the store or Toss, or contact us (section 6).
- **Missing items:** if you paid and the item did not arrive, contact us (section 6). An order number helps us check quickly.
