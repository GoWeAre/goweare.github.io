# I Had A Sword — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-07**
**Effective: 2026-10-07**

---

## 1. Overview

I Had A Sword (Korean title: 검터진다; "the game") is a mobile game about enhancing and growing swords. The game is operated by GoWeAre ("we", "the operator"). We collect only the information needed to provide the game.

This policy explains what the game collects and why, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. It applies to both the Android and iOS versions.

## 2. Accounts

The game **does not ask for your name, phone number, date of birth, or address.**

When you first launch the game, an account is created in one of these ways.

| Sign-in method | What is stored |
|---|---|
| Guest | A random identifier generated by the game. It is stored on your device and does not reveal who you are |
| Google account (Android, iOS) | Google account identifier, email address, and the name and language setting Google provides at sign-in |
| Google Play Games (Android) | Play Games player identifier and Play Games display name |
| Apple account (iOS, Sign in with Apple) | Apple user identifier. We do not request your name or email |

A guest account cannot be carried over if you change devices or uninstall the game. If you link a Google or Apple account in Settings, you can continue on another device. Linking is optional; every feature works without it.

Your email address is used only to identify and recover your account. It is not shown to other players and is not used for marketing.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information you enter

| Item | Purpose | Visible to other players? |
|---|---|---|
| Nickname | Shown in rankings, arena, friends, and guilds | Yes |
| Country | Shown next to your nickname; country rankings | Yes |
| Guild name, guild notice | Guild features | Yes — the name to all players, the notice to guild members |
| Text you add to a share image | Creating an image to share | Only to people you choose to share with |
| Coupon codes | Granting rewards | No |

Your nickname and guild name are **public**. Do not include your real name, contact details, or anything that identifies you.

The game first suggests a country estimated from your IP address, and you choose and confirm it yourself. The IP address itself is not stored for this purpose.

### 3.2 Game progress

To provide the game, we store the following on our servers.

- Swords you own and their enhancement levels, currencies (gold, gems, magic stones, etc.), runes, altar, achievements, quests, attendance, and adventure records
- Arena score, battle records, and ranking position
- Friend list, friend requests, and friend cheer records
- Guild membership, guild contributions, and raid participation
- Mail and reward claim records
- Ad reward claim records and the validity period of the ad removal pass
- Last login time and the game version in use

Of these, your nickname, country, main sword and its enhancement level, arena score, and guild membership are **visible to other players** in rankings, the arena, friend lists, and guild screens.

### 3.3 Purchase records

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. **The game never receives or stores your card number or other payment method details.**

To verify a purchase and deliver the item, we store:

- The purchase receipt and order number issued by the store, the product identifier, purchase time, currency, and amount
- A record linking the order number to your game account — used to confirm refunds

If you receive a refund from the store, the store notifies us. We review this information to prevent refund abuse, and may restrict use of the game if a policy violation is confirmed.

### 3.4 Information collected automatically

- **Device information** — When signing in to the game server (PlayFab), device specifications such as device model, operating system and version, and screen size are sent. This is used for troubleshooting and compatibility.
- **Access logs** — When the game communicates with our servers, your IP address and request time are recorded in the server provider's logs. These are used for security and incident response.
- **Advertising identifier (Google AdMob)** — To show ads, prevent invalid clicks, and measure ad performance, your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA if you allow tracking), an approximate region estimated from your IP address, and device information are sent to Google. You can delete or reset the advertising ID or turn off tracking in your device settings.
- **Engine diagnostics (Unity)** — Device and diagnostic information may be sent to Unity Technologies while the game engine and purchasing module operate.

### 3.5 Ads and consent

- Ads in the game play **only when you choose** to watch one for a reward (rewarded ads).
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads through **Google's consent message (UMP)** before loading ads. You can change your choice later in the game's Settings.
- On iOS, before loading ads the game asks for permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) through Apple's **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)** prompt. Every feature works if you decline; in that case non-personalized ads are shown.
- If you purchase the ad removal pass, you receive rewards without watching ads.

### 3.6 Information stored only on your device

Settings such as language and sound, the guest account identifier, scheduled notifications, and some "already shown" flags are stored **only on your device** and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

**We do not use a push server.** All notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device, and no remote push token is collected.

### 3.7 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location (GPS), contacts, the contents of your photos, camera or microphone, or health information.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed | If you decline |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Game notifications such as enhancement recharge and raids | You will not receive notifications |
| App tracking (iOS) | Used for personalized ads | Non-personalized ads are shown |
| Add to Photos | Saving a share image to your photo album | You can share the image but not save it |

**You can use the game even if you decline every permission.** The game does not read existing photos in your library.

## 6. Information visible to other players

The game has features you share with other players, such as rankings, the arena, friends, and guilds. In these features your **nickname, country, main sword and its enhancement level, arena score, and guild membership** are visible to other players. Your email address, purchase records, and device information are not.

The game contains links to external communities (Naver Cafe, Discord). After you follow a link, your information is handled under that service's policy.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- Your account and game progress are kept **until you delete your account.**
- When you delete your account, the account identifier, linked Google/Apple account information, nickname, country, game progress, friend list, and ranking records stored on our servers are deleted. Deletion starts as soon as you request it and may take some time to complete. A deleted account cannot be restored.
- If a guild leader deletes their account, the guild passes to another member, or is disbanded if there are no other members.
- The following records remain after account deletion.
  - **Purchase records:** The link between purchase order numbers and the account identifier at the time, and purchase receipt validation records, are kept for **5 years** as records of payment and supply of goods under the Republic of Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and are then deleted. They are used only for legal retention and refund confirmation.
  - Purchase records held by Google and Apple are subject to their own policies.
- Access logs held by server providers are deleted automatically under each provider's retention policy.

## 8. Your rights

You may at any time:

- **Access and correct** — You can change your nickname in the game.
- **Delete** — You can delete your account and records in the game under **Settings > Delete Account** (except the records noted in §7). If you have already uninstalled the game, contact us at the address below.
- **Withdraw consent** — Turning off a permission in your device settings stops the related collection. In regions where the consent message applies, you can change your ad consent in the game's Settings.
- **Opt out of personalized ads** — Reset your advertising ID or limit tracking in your device settings.

## 9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child's information has been collected, we delete it without delay.

## 10. Security

- All communication with our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Important operations such as granting currency, enhancement results, and purchase validation are performed on the server.
- Only the operator holds server administration rights, and administrative secret keys are not included in the game app.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will notify you through an in-game notice or an update. Material changes are announced at least 7 days before they take effect.
