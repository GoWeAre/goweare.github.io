# BedSloth — Paid features and refunds

**Last updated: 2026-10-09**
**Effective date: 2026-10-09**

---

**App:** BedSloth: Zero Pedometer

Everything sold in BedSloth is bought through App Store or Google Play billing. The stores
handle payment and refunds; this page explains what we sell and how refunds work.

## 1. What we sell

| Item | Type | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Skip Today Pass | One-time purchase, used up when redeemed | One pass. Redeeming it waives that day's step limit. You can hold up to 5. |
| Streak Saver | One-time purchase, used up when redeemed | One saver. Keeps your streak from breaking on a failed day. |
| BedSloth Premium | Auto-renewing subscription (1 month or 1 year) | No full-screen ads after fail alerts, one skip pass every week, and Premium-only decor you can get with fluff |

Prices are shown on the purchase buttons in your local currency, as set by the store.
Fluff (in-app points) and decor obtained with fluff are not paid items and have no cash value.

## 2. Subscription renewal and cancellation

- Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.
- The subscription renews automatically for the period you chose (every month or every year)
  at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- Cancel in the store's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  - iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions
  - Android: Google Play > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions
- After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. We
  do not refund the remaining part of a period (except for the withdrawal right in section 3).

## 3. Withdrawal and refund rules

- **Unused Skip Today Passes and Streak Savers:** you may withdraw from the purchase and get a
  full refund within 7 days of buying.
- **Passes and savers you have already used:** they take effect the moment you use them and
  cannot be undone, so the purchase can no longer be withdrawn.
- **Subscription:** within 7 days of payment you may withdraw if you have not used the benefits
  that came with it (the weekly skip pass, Premium-only decor). After that, the store's refund
  policy applies.
- If an item is not as described, or you paid but did not receive it, contact us regardless of
  the periods above. If we confirm it was not delivered, we will deliver it again or help you
  get a refund.
- If a minor made a purchase without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian may
  cancel it.
- Where the consumer law of your country gives you stronger rights, those rights apply.

## 4. How to request a refund

Refunds are processed by the store you paid through.

- **App Store (iPhone):** only Apple can issue refunds. Choose the purchase at
  https://reportaproblem.apple.com and submit a request.
- **Google Play (Android):** within 48 hours of purchase you can request a refund directly from
  your Google Play order history. After that, email us your order number (it starts with GPA)
  and we will handle it under the rules in section 3.

## 5. What changes after a refund

- Refunded passes and savers are removed from your wallet. If you already used them, the next
  one you buy of the same item is deducted first (the app tells you near the purchase button).
- If a subscription is refunded, Premium ends at that point. We do not take back the weekly skip
  passes you received or the Premium-only decor you got before then.

## 6. Deleting your account

Deleting your account also deletes any remaining passes, savers and decor; they cannot be
restored or refunded afterwards. A subscription is separate from your account, so cancel it in
the store as well.
