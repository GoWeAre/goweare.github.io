# BedSloth — Delete Your Account and Data

**Last updated: 2026-10-05**

> This is a translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

---

**App:** BedSloth: Zero Pedometer (침대늘보)

BedSloth doesn't ask you to sign up. It stores your records under an **anonymous account**
created the first time you open the app. You can delete this account and the data stored on
our server at any time.

## 1. Delete inside the app (recommended)

1. Open BedSloth.
2. Go to the **My Room** tab in the bottom bar.
3. Tap the **gear (Settings)** icon in the top right.
4. At the bottom, under **Delete account**, tap **Delete my account**.
5. Confirm in the dialog. Deletion happens immediately.

After deletion, the app starts over with a new anonymous account.

## 2. If you've already removed the app or can't open it

Email us to request deletion.

- **Email:** go.we.are.official@gmail.com
- **Subject:** BedSloth account deletion request
- **Please include:** the **email address of the Google or Apple account you linked** in the app

We will verify the request and delete your data within **10 days**,
then reply to confirm.

> If you never linked a Google or Apple account, we cannot tell which anonymous records are yours once
> the app is removed, so we can't single them out for deletion. Those records contain no
> identity information such as your name or email. If you still have the device, please use
> method 1 above before reinstalling (reinstalling creates a new anonymous account).

## 3. Data that is deleted

Deleting your account **immediately and permanently** deletes the following from our server
(except the remaining records listed in section 4):

- Your anonymous account identifier and (if linked) the Google / Apple account link, including the email address
- Nickname, character (avatar) and daily step limits
- Daily step totals and their source (device sensor / health data)
- Streaks, badges and achievements, Streak Savers and postpone passes owned/used, and exemption records
- Quest completions and fluff, decoration purchases and equipped decorations
- Friendships, block records, and blankets sent and received
- Blanket party membership and party invitations sent and received
- Your invite code
- Reports you made and reports about you
- Ranking information

Deletion cannot be undone.

## 4. Data that is not deleted or is kept separately

- **Invite reward records:** kept so the other person keeps the reward they already received and
  the invite reward limit still works, but your deleted account's identifier is removed so the
  record no longer shows whose it was.
- **Blanket parties:** if other members remain in a party you created, the party continues for
  them and only the "created by" information is removed.
- **Payment records (BedSloth server):** in-app payment notification records (event ID, product,
  the account identifier string at the time, time) are kept for **5 years** as records of payment
  and supply of goods under Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and
  then deleted. They are unlinked from your account, but the account identifier string at the time
  remains.
- **Ad reward verification records:** records of watching an ad to postpone today (ad reward
  transaction ID, date, step count, result, time) are kept for support and to prevent double
  rewards, but your deleted account's identifier is removed so the record no longer shows
  whose it was.
- **Purchase records:** In-app purchase and subscription history may be kept for the legally
  required period under the policies of Google Play / the App Store and our purchase management
  service (RevenueCat). Deleting your account **does not cancel a subscription** — please cancel
  it first in Google Play / App Store subscription settings.
- **Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase):** Collected in a form that does not identify
  you and automatically deleted after each service's retention period.
  (crash reports 90 days, user-level usage statistics at most 14 months)
- **Data on your device:** Settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Health data:** BedSloth never writes to Health Connect/HealthKit, so there is nothing to delete
  there. You can revoke the app's read access in Health Connect or your device settings.
