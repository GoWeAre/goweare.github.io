# My Freesia — Delete Your Account and Data

**Last updated: 2026-10-27**

> This is a translation. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

---

**App:** My Freesia (마이 프리지아)

My Freesia syncs the records of users who sign in with an Apple, Google or Kakao account to our server.
You can delete this account and the data stored on our server at any time. If you use guest mode
("Browse"), nothing is stored on our server (see section 5).

## 1. Delete inside the app (recommended)

1. Open My Freesia and make sure you are signed in.
2. Pull up or tap the menu at the bottom of the screen to open it.
3. Tap the **gear (Settings)** icon.
4. At the very bottom of Settings, tap **Delete Account**.
5. In the "Delete your account?" dialog, tap **Continue**.
6. Type **delete** in the box, then tap the **delete** button.
7. If an Apple account is linked, an Apple confirmation sheet appears once more. Confirming it also asks
   Apple to disconnect the app (revoke the token). Your account is deleted even if you close this sheet.

When deletion is finished, the records on this device are removed as well and the app returns to the
start screen.

## 2. If you've already removed the app or can't open it

Email us to request deletion.

- **Email:** go.we.are.official@gmail.com
- **Subject:** My Freesia account deletion request
- **Please include:** how you signed in (Apple, Google or Kakao) and that account's email address (for
  Kakao, your Kakao nickname). If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", give the @privaterelay.appleid.com
  address.

After verifying that the request is yours, we will delete the account within **10 days** and reply with
the result.

> If you can reinstall the app, you can sign in with the same account and delete it right away using
> section 1.

## 3. Data that is deleted

When you delete your account, the following data on our server is deleted **immediately and permanently**.

- Your account and the linked Apple, Google and Kakao account information (including email address, name,
  profile photo address and nickname)
- To-dos, events, challenges and their sticker logs, mandala chart boards and cells
- Daily notes (diary), weekly goals, challenge notes, today's moods, D-days
- Diary photos and the photo list
- Any of the records above that you had deleted in the app and that were only marked as deleted
- Carrot earning and spending records, and your pet yard's decoration layout
- Purchase verification records (entitlements)
- Device push tokens and scheduled event reminders

The records stored on this device are deleted as well. Deletion cannot be undone.

## 4. Data that is not deleted or is kept separately

- **Subscriptions:** deleting your account does **not cancel** your App Store subscription automatically.
  Cancel it first in iPhone **Settings > your name > Subscriptions**.
- **Purchase history:** your payment history is kept by Apple, which processed the payment, under Apple's
  policies. Lifetime and theme purchases made with the same Apple account can be applied again on a new
  account with **Restore Purchases**.
- **Access logs:** access and error logs at our server provider (Supabase) are deleted automatically when
  their retention period ends. Until then, a record of the account deletion may remain.
- **Data on your device:** the app lock passcode (in the device Keychain), Home Screen widget data and
  similar data are removed when you delete the app.
- **Files you already shared:** PDF diaries and recap cards you exported and shared yourself are outside
  the app and are not deleted.

## 5. If you use guest mode

Guest-mode records exist only on this device, not on our server. They are deleted when you delete the app
or use **Settings > Reset All Data**.
