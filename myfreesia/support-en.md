# My Freesia Support

**App:** My Freesia (마이 프리지아)

## Contact

- **Email:** go.we.are.official@gmail.com
- Telling us your device (for example, iPhone 15), iOS version and how you sign in (Apple, Google, Kakao
  or guest) helps us help you faster. We reply within 10 days.

## Frequently asked questions

### I can't see the Premium or theme I bought

1. Make sure you are signed in with the account you used when buying. Purchases are applied to the
   signed-in account.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases** in the **Shop** or on the Premium screen.
3. If you restore the same App Store purchase on a different account, the entitlement moves to the
   account that restored it.
4. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the order number from Apple's receipt email.

### I want to cancel my subscription or get a refund

- Cancel: in iPhone **Settings > your name > Subscriptions**. Deleting the app or your account does not
  cancel it.
- Refund: only Apple can issue refunds. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
- For details, see [Paid features and refunds](refund-policy-en.md).

### How are my records saved and synced?

- **When signed in:** records are saved on your device and automatically backed up and synced to our
  server when you are online. You can continue on another device signed in to the same account, and your
  records come back when you reinstall the app and sign in. Anything you write offline is uploaded when
  you reconnect.
- **If this device looks different from the server:** tap **Settings > Restore from server** to bring
  back the copy saved on the server. Records not yet uploaded are not erased.
- **Guest mode:** records are saved only on this device and are not backed up to the server. Deleting the
  app removes them, so sign in to keep your records safe. When you sign in, what you wrote as a guest moves
  into that account.

### I forgot my app lock passcode

Tap **Forgot passcode** on the lock screen.

- **When signed in:** tap **Sign out and remove lock**. Records on this device are erased, but they're
  saved on the server and come back when you sign in again. If some records haven't been uploaded yet, the
  app asks you to connect to the internet and try again.
- **Guest mode:** your records are only on this device, so removing the lock with **Erase all records and
  remove lock** erases all of them, and this can't be undone.

### I want to delete my account

You can delete it right away in **Settings > Delete Account**. For the steps and what gets deleted, see
[Delete your account](account-deletion-en.md).
